Summer In The City 2026

This year, I was back at Summer In The City which was based in Glasgow Clyde College @ Anniesland for my 3rd summer as their Technical Director. Click on each show page to know what I got up to, or read more about the camp below.

THE TECHNICAL SET UP

The set-up for Summer In The City's 2026 summer programme was as follows:

LIGHTING

Control: EOS Console (2.9)
Fixtures: 6x Moving Wash Lights | 2x Moving Spot Lights | 1x 5m Pixel Tape | 2x MicroPar Bars
Notes: All fixtures were controlled via wireless DMX and networking, with advanced console pre-programming and system setup completed ahead of the programme.

SOUND

Control: Behringer X32 Producer
Inputs: 2x SFX Inputs | 6x Mic Packs | 6x Handheld Microphones
PA Setup: 2x Main PA Speakers | 1x Backing Speaker for Soundscape | 1x Monitor System

STAGING

4x staging platforms, each 3ft tall and measuring 2m x 1m, were used throughout the programme. The staging was fully rearranged each week to meet the director's requirements, with all platforms safety marked and securely fixed in position.

CABLING

Over 100 cables were used throughout the project, including cable looms, runs from control positions to the stage, and fixture-to-fixture patching.

ABOUT THE CAMP

Summer In The City is a holiday-camp ran by Nicola Pitticas, which has venues all over Glasgow. The Theatre & Drama programme has been running for 3 years now. Each week, a new theme is introduced and the director (SC/LC) will write a script, and give it to the children. Throughout the week, they get to learn what it's like to be part of a show—to play theatre games, to get to know theatre, and what it's like to put on a play. Then, they begin rehearsals for the play which is dynamically cast so everyone gets the chance to play a lead role. They get a dress rehearsal on a Friday morning, and 1 performance on a Friday afternoon to parents, family, friends, and guardians. No kids were involved in any of the technical aspects this year. For more questions, please contact Nicola Pitticas at Summer In The City.
