Trolls

Date: 20th to 24th of July, 2026

Programme Week: 3

Role: Technical Director/SFX Operator

About the Show

For the third week of the programme we did Trolls. This week was a fun week, as we got to encorporate loads of additional elements. This week, we individually mic'ed up kids and had a full mic plot with backstage assistance helping to make sure every kid got a mic with the dynamic casting in place. The sound was a full plot, with over 20 cues as well as a full sound desk of cues and labelling to ensure that I was able to operate the show as easily and efficently as possible. This week we had a new set of hands with LX operation, CR who joined us on the Friday. The show was a tech-heavy show and SFX was programmed on the Wednesday and LX was programmed over the course of thursday and friday with around a total of 90 to 110 cues. This week there was lots of movement, effects, and colourful sequences involved to make the show an eye-catching, exciting show to watch. We also explored further with transitions, and this week we had our largest number of kids. It was a long and hard week, but a really good one and I had the ability to explore and really get to grips with the show and put in everything that we had and ensure that every cue was spot on and that the show was this eyecatching spectical that the director wanted it to be. We did face some small issues with the mic plot in the beginnging of the show, however at the end it was all fixed and this was due to the short transition times and dynamic casting.

About the Programme

Summer In The City is a holiday-camp ran by Nicola Pitticas, which has venues all over Glasgow. The Theatre & Drama programme has been running for 3 years now. Each week, a new theme is introduced and the director (SC/LC) will write a script, and give it to the children. Throughout the week, they get to learn what its like to be part of a show. To play theatre games, to get to know theatre, and what its like to out on a play. Then, they begin rehearsals for the play which is dynamically cast so everyone gets the chance to play a lead role. They get a dress rehearsal on a friday morning, and 1 preformance on a friday afternoon to parents, family, friends, and guardians. No kids were involved in any of the technical aspects this year. For more questions, please contact Nicola Pitticas at Summer In The City.

Technical Setup

The set-up for Summer In The City's 2026 summer programme was as follows:

LIGHTING

Control: EOS Console (2.9)
Fixtures: 6x Moving Wash Lights | 2x Moving Spot Lights | 1x 5m Pixel Tape | 2x MicroPar Bars
Notes: All fixtures were controlled via wireless DMX and networking, with advanced console pre-programming and system setup completed ahead of the programme.

SOUND

Control: Behringer X32 Producer
Inputs: 2x SFX Inputs | 6x Mic Packs | 6x Handheld Microphones
PA Setup: 2x Main PA Speakers | 1x Backing Speaker for Soundscape | 1x Monitor System

STAGING

4x staging platforms, each 3ft tall and measuring 2m x 1m, were used throughout the programme. The staging was fully rearranged each week to meet the director's requirements, with all platforms safety marked and securely fixed in position.

CABLING

Over 100 cables were used throughout the project, including cable looms, runs from control positions to the stage, and fixture-to-fixture patching.
