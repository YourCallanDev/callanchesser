Wonderboy

Date: October 1st, 2026

Role: Sound Designer

About the Workshop

For this Nat5 Production Skills piece, I was part of the production team as the Sound designer. This intaled designing the full sound plot for a show in one of our schools drama studios. The process took place for 3 periods over several weeks, where I completed research about the composer, the themes and moods, created a MultiPlay showfile, filled in all the Nat5 level paperwork, annoated a full script, and operated a show with a selection of pre-show music to match the themes of the show. Overall, I found this process really helpful as it allowed me to experience what my actual Nat5 will be like!
